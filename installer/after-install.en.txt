Nunchi is installed.

Getting started
  Choose "Nunchi" under Korean with Win+Space. Apps that are already open use Nunchi once they are restarted.
  Type on your English keyboard as usual: Nunchi tells English from Korean word by word and puts in the right letters.

Keys
  The Han/Yeong key, right Alt (pressed and released on its own), Shift+Esc
    Switch the word being typed, or the word just committed, to the other language.
    Switching the same word the same way twice teaches Nunchi that word.
  The Hanja key
    Pauses or resumes Nunchi in this text field (while paused, keys go in as typed).

Taskbar
  In the input indicator, "가" means on, and "가" with a small red x means off.
  A click pauses or resumes it; a right click opens its menu (off in this app, settings).

Settings
  Open "Nunchi Settings" from the Start menu, or the taskbar icon's menu.
  Layouts, keys, rules for apps and the words Nunchi learned can be changed there.

If something goes wrong
  Turn on Settings > 정보 > "진단 기록 남기기", make it happen again, and send the log file from
  %APPDATA%\Nunchi\logs along. The log never holds what you type.

Nunchi is an unofficial Windows port based on Araso for macOS (nyttastudio).
